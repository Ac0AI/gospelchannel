## Design context

### Users

People comparing nearby churches before a Sunday visit, often on mobile. They need a calm, scannable shortlist with enough practical detail to decide what to verify next.

### Brand personality

Warm, editorial, welcoming, and grounded. GospelChannel should feel like a trusted church guide, not a review marketplace or a software dashboard.

### Aesthetic direction

Use the existing light GospelChannel system: linen backgrounds, white list surfaces, espresso typography, rose-gold actions, soft warm borders, and restrained shadows. Dark brown is for type and occasional emphasis, not large interface panels.

### Design principles

- Make church comparison fast to scan on both mobile and desktop.
- Reuse the established church-card grid, including imagery and fallbacks, across the site.
- Keep distance, service time, visitor details, and freshness visible without crowding the church name.
- Reserve rose-gold for hierarchy, active states, and primary actions.
- Keep uncertainty and source freshness explicit without making the experience feel defensive.
